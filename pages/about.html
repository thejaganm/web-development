About Me

Hello! I'm Jagan Antony M,
an artist from Palakkad, Kerala, passionate about oil painting, figurative art, and creativity.
This blog is a space where I share my artistic journey, creative experiences, thoughts, inspiration, and things I discover along the way.
I believe art is more than just colours on a canvas; it's a way of expressing emotions, telling stories, and seeing the world differently.
Through this platform, I hope to connect with creative minds, inspire fellow artists, and share my passion with everyone who appreciates creativity.
